### The Mission
The current paradigm of artificial intelligence optimizes for associative pattern matching, resulting in systems that act as consensus engines rather than forensic reasoning engines. 
My work operates at the intersection of classical philosophy and machine learning, with the primary objective of forcing AI models to mathematically verify truth rather than generate probabilistic fabrication.
A fatal flaw in standard LLMs is their reliance on parametric memory. If you feed a model a specific text—such as a newly published book—it defaults to back to it's training. Rather than forensically analyzing the provided context-
the model bypasses the document to retrieve the patterns it memorized during pre-training. This renders its "deep thinking" and Chain of Thought meaningless; 
the model is simply generating a probabilistic defense of its own training bias, rendering the objective ground truth useless.
My architectures are designed to break this stubborn reliance on pre-trained consensus, forcing strict contextual grounding.


### Current Focus: Rene-Deskeptic
I am the architect behind **Rene-Deskeptic**, an LLM framework currently in the theoretical design phase. The architecture is engineered to enforce "metacognitive paranoia"
